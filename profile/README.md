# FastTradein

FastTradein is trade-in and buyback management software for businesses that buy used electronics: retailers, repair shops and device resellers. It connects website requests with in-person intake.

Customers get a preliminary device valuation based on your buyback prices and their answers about condition. Your team reviews requests, inspects devices, records their condition and IMEI or serial number, and confirms the final price. If inspection leads to a lower offer, the customer can accept or decline it through a link.

Manage requests and inspection records in one workspace, prepare handover documents and record payment status. Your business buys the devices and pays the customer.
